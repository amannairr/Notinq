# Notinq Release-Candidate Manual QA

This checklist covers the visual and interaction checks that could not be
reliably completed in the current automated environment. Keep the product
principle in view throughout:

> AI proposes. Student decides.

## Appearance

- Launch Notinq in light appearance.
- Launch Notinq in dark appearance.
- Verify readable contrast for editor text, secondary text, borders, cards,
  popovers, disabled controls, proposal preview, Study Map, Knowledge Gaps,
  AI Provenance, and Learning Insights.

## Window Sizes

- Test a comfortable default window.
- Test a narrow window.
- Test a wide window.
- Verify the sidebar, middle panel, editor, toolbar, proposal preview, Study Map,
  and Learning Insights avoid clipping, overlapping, or awkward stretching.

## Editor And Proposal Flow

- Open or create a note with ordinary study content.
- Edit text normally.
- Select text and use the AI selection toolbar.
- Generate an AI proposal.
- Confirm the document does not change before Accept.
- Inspect proposal title, scope, action, adaptive/source/gap metadata, and
  Accept/Edit/Reject hierarchy.
- Edit the proposal, then Accept it.
- Undo the accepted insertion and verify the document and selection remain sane.
- Repeat the flow with Reject and verify the note is unchanged.

## AI Provenance

- Accept AI-generated content.
- Open AI Provenance for the accepted content.
- Verify the popover/card explains that the content was generated by AI, which
  action produced it, and whether context/source details are available.
- Confirm no internal IDs, raw instrumentation data, or private note excerpts are
  exposed unnecessarily.
- Confirm the affordance is subtle and does not visually dominate the editor.

## Study

- Open Study before analysis has run.
- Start analysis and inspect the loading state.
- Inspect Study Map after analysis with no gaps.
- Inspect Study Map after analysis with one or more Knowledge Gaps.
- Verify Coverage, Knowledge Gaps, Teach Me, and Practice feel connected rather
  than like unrelated panels.

## Knowledge Gaps

- Open a detected gap.
- Use Explain and verify an AI proposal appears instead of direct insertion.
- Resolve a gap and confirm it stays resolved after ordinary reanalysis.
- Ignore a gap and confirm it stays ignored.
- Check empty, resolved, ignored, and active gap states.

## Teach Me

- Start Teach Me from Study.
- Inspect the question, answer input, feedback/learning state, next-question
  affordance, and completion state.
- Verify the flow feels based on the current note and learning context, not like
  an unrelated chat experience.

## Learning Insights

- Inspect initial, loading, success, and failure/retry states.
- Check covered, partially covered, missing concepts, and review priorities.
- Use any generated-content action and verify wording says it will propose adding
  to the note, not silently insert.
- Check long concept names and dense results at narrow width.

## Accessibility And Interaction

- Navigate major controls with the keyboard.
- Verify Return/Space activation where expected.
- Verify Escape dismisses popovers or transient UI where expected.
- Check VoiceOver labels for proposal preview, Accept, Edit, Reject, Study Map,
  Knowledge Gap actions, Teach Me, Learning Insights, mode controls, and settings.
- Confirm disabled controls remain understandable.
