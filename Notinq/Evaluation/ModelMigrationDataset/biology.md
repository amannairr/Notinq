# Biology: Cellular Respiration and Enzymes

Cells convert glucose into usable energy through cellular respiration.
The process begins with glycolysis in the cytoplasm, continues in the mitochondria, and produces ATP.
Enzymes speed up metabolic reactions without being consumed.
Key concepts:
- cellular respiration
- glycolysis
- mitochondria
- ATP
- enzymes

Relationships:
- glycolysis is part of cellular respiration
- mitochondria support ATP production
- enzymes catalyze metabolic reactions
